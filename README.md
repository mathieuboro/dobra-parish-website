# Dobra Parish Website

A custom-designed website for the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin
Mary Parish in Dobra, Poland.

This repository showcases the project, its design and features.
The source code is maintained privately under the parish's GitHub account.

The project combines a distinctive visual identity with practical
information used by the parish community: Mass times, announcements,
parish office hours and guidance on the sacraments.

[Visit the live website](https://parafianmp-dobra.pl/)

## Design and audience

The interface was designed for visitors of different ages and levels
of digital confidence. The main priorities are readability, clear
navigation and straightforward access to frequently needed information.

Key design choices include:

- **Large, prominent headings** that help visitors recognise sections
  and find their way through the page.
- **Readable typography and clear spacing** that make information
  easier to scan.
- **Prominent dates, times and locations**, especially for Masses,
  services and parish announcements.
- **A restrained colour palette** combining warm backgrounds,
  dark text and gold accents.
- **A custom illustration of the actual parish church**, giving
  the website a visual identity connected to the local community.
- **Layouts adapted to different screen sizes**, including a compact
  mobile calendar and simpler arrangements of links and information
  on smaller screens.

## Content and structure

The homepage brings together the information visitors are most likely
to need regularly: the liturgical calendar, Mass times, parish office
details, current announcements and news.

Dedicated pages provide more detail about:

- Masses, services and confession
- Parish announcements and Mass intentions
- News and community events
- Sacraments and practical preparation
- Parish office hours and contact information
- The parish, its history and local traditions
- The parish cemetery

This structure keeps everyday information easy to reach while giving
longer articles and historical material their own space.

## Interactive features

- An animated SVG church illustration with a real-time clock
- Clickable church doors that open a view of the interior
- A liturgical calendar with day selection and daily readings links
- Announcement scans that visitors can open and enlarge
- Seasonal presentation and notices for selected celebrations

## Content management

Pages CMS allows parish staff to update announcements, news and
other content without editing the website's source code.

Content is stored in GitHub, processed by a Node.js build script
and published as static pages through Cloudflare Pages.

## How it was built

The website was created with AI tools supporting design exploration,
coding and testing.

My role includes:

- Defining the project's requirements and priorities
- Shaping the visual direction and content structure
- Reviewing and refining proposed designs and interactions
- Coordinating feedback with the parish
- Managing deployment and ongoing updates

The project continues to evolve through practical feedback and
improvements to its everyday usability.

## Technology

- HTML, CSS and JavaScript
- Node.js for static site generation
- Pages CMS for content editing
- GitHub for source and content storage
- Cloudflare Pages for hosting and deployment
